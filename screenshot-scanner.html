Understanding Screenshot Analysis

What Screenshot Analysis Does

Our analyzer looks for common scam patterns and warning signs in screenshots, including urgency language, financial requests, credential harvesting, and phishing indicators.

Common Warning Signs

  • Urgency: "Act now," "Last chance," "Immediate action required"
  • Financial Requests: "Send money," "Pay fee," "Deposit first"
  • Credential Requests: "Password," "OTP," "PIN," "CVV"
  • Phishing: Fake verification, suspicious login requests
  • Offer Scams: Unrealistic discounts, guaranteed profits

Limitations

Screenshot analysis cannot definitively prove content is fake or malicious. It identifies potential warning signs based on patterns. Always verify information independently through official channels.

🔒

Your Privacy

Screenshot analysis is performed locally in your browser. We do not store or transmit the screenshots you upload to external servers.
