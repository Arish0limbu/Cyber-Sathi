About Cyber Sathi

Your Digital Safety Companion

Our Mission

Cyber Sathi (साथी) means "Cyber Companion" in Hindi. We believe everyone deserves access to tools that help them navigate the digital world safely. Our mission is to provide accessible, easy-to-use tools that help ordinary users identify and understand potential online threats before they cause harm.

We focus on education and risk analysis rather than making unrealistic guarantees. Our tools help users make informed decisions by highlighting potential warning signs and providing actionable recommendations.

What We Do

🔗

URL Analysis

Analyze suspicious URLs for potential security risks including phishing indicators, suspicious domains, and known threat patterns.

📱

QR Code Protection

Scan and analyze QR codes to reveal hidden destinations and check for malicious links before you click.

🖼

Screenshot Analysis

Upload screenshots of suspicious content to detect warning signs like urgency, financial requests, and credential harvesting.

💬

Message Checking

Analyze suspicious messages for scam indicators including urgency, threats, financial requests, and impersonation attempts.

🛒

Store Safety

Check online shopping websites for potential fraud indicators and suspicious patterns.

🎓

Education

Learn about common scams, phishing techniques, and how to protect yourself through our comprehensive scam library and safety tips.

Our Approach

🔍

Pattern Recognition

Our tools analyze content for known scam patterns and warning indicators, helping identify potential threats.

📚

Education First

We believe in empowering users with knowledge. Every analysis includes explanations and educational context.

⚖️

Responsible Detection

We never claim 100% accuracy. Our results are based on observable indicators and include clear recommendations.

🔒

Privacy Focused

Analysis happens locally in your browser where possible. We don't store your data or transmit it unnecessarily.

Our Privacy Commitment

🔒

Local Processing

We process information locally in your browser whenever possible to protect your privacy.

🗑️

No Data Storage

We don't permanently store the URLs, messages, or content you analyze.

🛡️

No Credential Collection

We never ask for or collect passwords, OTPs, PINs, or banking credentials.

📤

No Automatic Uploads

Screenshots and files are only analyzed when you explicitly choose to upload them.

⚠️

Important Disclaimer

Cyber Sathi provides educational tools and analysis based on observable patterns. Automated analysis cannot guarantee that a website, QR code, screenshot, or message is safe or malicious. Our tools should be used as one part of your decision-making process, not as the sole basis for security decisions.

Always verify information independently through official channels. If you believe you have been victimized by a scam, contact relevant authorities and financial institutions immediately.

Our Technology

Cyber Sathi is built as a GitHub Pages-compatible frontend-only application using:

  • HTML5, CSS3, and Vanilla JavaScript - No heavy frameworks or dependencies
  • Browser APIs - Leveraging native browser capabilities for analysis
  • Local Processing - Analysis happens client-side for privacy
  • Pattern Recognition - Identifying known scam patterns and indicators
  • Educational Scoring - Risk scores based on observable factors

This architecture ensures the platform is accessible, fast, and works without requiring backend infrastructure or user registration.

Our Vision

We envision a world where everyone has access to tools that help them navigate the digital landscape safely. As cyber threats continue to evolve, we're committed to:

  • Expanding our detection capabilities to cover emerging threat types
  • Improving our educational content to help users understand new scams
  • Enhancing our tools while maintaining privacy and accessibility
  • Building a community around digital safety education
