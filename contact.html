Frequently Asked Questions

Is Cyber Sathi free to use?

Yes, Cyber Sathi is completely free to use. We believe digital safety tools should be accessible to everyone.

Is my data safe when using your tools?

We prioritize privacy and process information locally in your browser whenever possible. We don't store URLs, messages, or content you analyze.

Can Cyber Sathi guarantee a website is safe?

No. Our analysis is based on observable patterns and indicators. We never claim 100% accuracy. Always verify information independently.

How can I report a new scam pattern?

You can email us at report@cybersathi.com with details about the scam. While we can't respond to every report, we appreciate the information.

Can I use Cyber Sathi commercially?

Cyber Sathi is designed for individual educational use. For commercial or enterprise applications, please contact us to discuss licensing options.

How do I cite Cyber Sathi in research or publications?

Please contact us at info@cybersathi.com for citation guidelines and permissions for academic or research use.
