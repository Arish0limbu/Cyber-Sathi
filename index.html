Safety Tips

🔒

Verify URLs

Always check the domain name carefully before entering credentials

📱

Scan QR Codes

Use a QR scanner to check where the code leads before clicking

🚫

Never Share OTPs

Legitimate organizations will never ask for your OTP

⏰

Watch for Urgency

Scammers create false urgency to make you act without thinking

💰

Too Good to Be True

If an offer seems unrealistic, it probably is

📞

Verify Independently

Contact organizations through official channels, not links in messages
