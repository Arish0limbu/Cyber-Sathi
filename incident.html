Emergency Incident Guide

Immediate steps to take if you think you've been targeted by a scam

🚨

Act Quickly

If you believe you've been scammed or compromised, time is critical. Follow the relevant steps below immediately.

I Clicked a Suspicious Link I Entered My Password I Shared an OTP I Sent Money to a Scammer I Installed Suspicious Software My Account Was Compromised
🔗

I Clicked a Suspicious Link

1

Close the Page Immediately

Close the tab or window without entering any information.

2

Do Not Enter Any Information

If the page is asking for login details, personal information, or payment details, do not provide them.

3

Clear Your Browser Cache

Clear your browser's cache and cookies to remove any potential malicious scripts.

4

Run a Security Scan

Run a full security scan on your device using reputable antivirus software.

5

Monitor Your Accounts

Keep an eye on your accounts for suspicious activity over the next few weeks.

🔐

I Entered My Password

1

Change Your Password Immediately

Go to the official website (not through the link) and change your password right away.

2

Enable Two-Factor Authentication

If you haven't already, enable 2FA on the compromised account and all other important accounts.

3

Check for Unauthorized Access

Review your account's recent activity and login history. Sign out all other sessions if possible.

4

Change Similar Passwords

If you use the same or similar password on other sites, change those passwords too.

5

Contact the Service Provider

Inform the service about the potential breach so they can monitor your account for suspicious activity.

6

Monitor for Identity Theft

Watch for suspicious activity on other accounts and consider placing a fraud alert on your credit reports.

Password Security Tips

I Shared an OTP

1

Contact Your Bank Immediately

Call your bank's official number (from the back of your card) to report the incident and freeze your account if necessary.

2

Block Cards and Accounts

If the OTP was related to banking or payments, block relevant cards and accounts to prevent unauthorized transactions.

3

Review Recent Transactions

Check your account statements for any unauthorized transactions and report them immediately.

4

Change Affected Passwords

If the OTP was for account access, change that password immediately and enable 2FA.

5

Report the Incident

Report the incident to your bank and relevant authorities. File a police report if money was stolen.

I Sent Money to a Scammer

1

Contact Your Payment Provider Immediately

Call your bank, credit card company, or payment platform right away to report the fraudulent transaction.

2

Request a Transaction Reversal

Ask your payment provider to reverse the transaction if possible. Act quickly, as time limits may apply.

3

File a Police Report

File a report with your local police. You may need this for insurance claims or to recover funds.

4

Report to Consumer Protection Agencies

Report the scam to relevant consumer protection agencies in your country.

5

Secure Your Accounts

Change passwords for any accounts that may have been compromised during the scam.

6

Monitor for Further Attempts

Scammers often target victims again. Be extremely cautious of any follow-up communications.

I Installed Suspicious Software

1

Disconnect from the Internet

Disconnect your device from the internet to prevent the malware from communicating with attackers or spreading.

2

Boot into Safe Mode

Restart your computer in safe mode to prevent the malware from running automatically.

3

Run a Full Security Scan

Use reputable antivirus software to run a full system scan and remove any detected threats.

4

Uninstall Suspicious Programs

Go to your control panel and uninstall any programs you don't recognize or that were installed around the time of the incident.

5

Change Your Passwords

Once your device is clean, change all your passwords, especially for sensitive accounts like banking and email.

6

Consider Professional Help

If you're unsure about removing the malware or if the problem persists, seek professional technical assistance.

Device Security Tips
👤

My Account Was Compromised

1

Change Your Password

Change your password immediately through the official website or app.

2

Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Set up 2FA to prevent unauthorized access even if your password is compromised.

3

Review Account Activity

Check recent login attempts, connected devices, and account changes. Remove any unrecognized access.

4

Check Connected Apps

Review and revoke access for any third-party apps or services you don't recognize.

5

Notify Your Contacts

If the attacker may have sent messages to your contacts, inform them that your account was compromised.

6

Contact Support

Report the compromise to the service's support team for further assistance.

Account Security Tips

Prevention for the Future

🔒

Use Strong, Unique Passwords

Never reuse passwords across different sites. Use a password manager to generate and store complex passwords.

📱

Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Turn on 2FA everywhere it's available. It's one of the most effective security measures.

🔍

Verify Before You Click

Always check URLs carefully and use scanning tools before visiting suspicious links.

🎓

Stay Educated

Keep learning about common scams and security best practices. Scammers constantly evolve their tactics.
