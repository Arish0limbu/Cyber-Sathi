Understanding Online Shopping Scams

Common Store Scam Signs

  • Unrealistic Prices: Prices that are too good to be true
  • Poor Website Design: Unprofessional or copied designs
  • Limited Contact Info: No physical address or phone number
  • Suspicious Payment Methods: Only accepting wire transfers or crypto
  • Fake Reviews: Generic or copied customer reviews
  • Urgency Tactics: Limited time offers to pressure buying

How to Verify a Store

  • Check for HTTPS and valid SSL certificate
  • Look for contact information and verify it
  • Search for reviews from independent sources
  • Check the domain registration age
  • Look for social media presence and activity
  • Verify the company through official business registries
  • Start with a small test purchase

Safe Shopping Practices

  • Use credit cards for fraud protection
  • Avoid stores that only accept wire transfers
  • Keep records of all transactions
  • Be cautious of new stores with no history
  • Trust your instincts if something feels wrong
  • Use reputable payment platforms when possible
