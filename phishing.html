Educational Examples Only

DEMO / EDUCATIONAL EXAMPLE
SecureBank
user@example.com
••••••••
Login
⚠️ Account will be suspended if not verified

Fake Login Page Analysis

🚩 Red Flags

  • Urgency: "Account will be suspended" creates false urgency
  • Unexpected Request: Why would you need to verify again?
  • Check Domain: Look at the actual URL, not just the logo
  • Spelling: Watch for subtle misspellings in the domain
  • HTTPS: Ensure the site has valid HTTPS

✅ What to Check

  • Verify the domain name matches the official website
  • Check for HTTPS and valid SSL certificate
  • Look for contact information on the official site
  • Contact the company through official channels
  • Never enter credentials based on an email link

SecureBank Alerts
+1 (555) 123-4567

ALERT: Your account has been limited due to suspicious activity. Immediate action required.

Click here to verify: http://securebank-verify.com/login

If not verified within 24 hours, your account will be permanently closed.

Today, 2:34 PM

Fake Bank Message Analysis

🚩 Red Flags

  • Threats: "Account will be permanently closed"
  • Urgency: "Immediate action required"
  • Suspicious Link: securebank-verify.com is not the official domain
  • Generic Greeting: No personalization
  • Unexpected: Did you request this verification?

✅ What to Do

  • Don't click the link in the message
  • Contact your bank through the official app or website
  • Call the official bank number from your card
  • Check your account through official channels
  • Report the message to your bank
DEMO / EDUCATIONAL EXAMPLE
SocialPlatform

Account Verification Required

Your account has been flagged for verification. To avoid permanent suspension, please verify your identity within 2 hours.

Verify Now

⚠️ Failure to verify will result in account loss

Fake Social Media Verification Analysis

🚩 Red Flags

  • False Urgency: "Within 2 hours" is unrealistic
  • Threat: "Permanent suspension" is a common threat
  • Unexpected: Real platforms don't demand urgent verification like this
  • Generic Message: Lacks specific account details
  • Suspicious Link: Don't click - verify through the app

✅ What to Do

  • Open the official app directly (not through links)
  • Check your account status in settings
  • Look for official notifications in the app
  • Contact support through the official app
  • Never share credentials through external links

General Phishing Protection Tips

🔍

Always Check URLs

Look carefully at the domain name. Scammers often use slight misspellings or different extensions.

⏰

Question Urgency

Real organizations rarely create false urgency. Take time to verify independently.

📞

Verify Independently

Contact organizations through official channels, not using information in suspicious messages.

🔐

Protect Credentials

Never enter passwords, OTPs, or PINs based on links in messages or emails.

🎯

Trust Your Instincts

If something feels wrong, it probably is. Better to be safe than sorry.

📱

Use Security Tools

Use URL scanners and message analyzers when you're unsure about content.
