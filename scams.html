QR Code Scams

Scammers use QR codes to hide malicious URLs that can steal your information or money. Always scan QR codes before clicking.

How it works:

  • Scammers place fake QR codes in public places
  • Send QR codes via text or email claiming to be payments
  • QR codes redirect to phishing sites or initiate payments

Protection:

  • Use a QR scanner that shows the destination
  • Verify where the code leads before interacting
  • Be suspicious of unsolicited QR codes

Payment Scams

Fraudulent payment requests that trick you into sending money or sharing payment details.

Common types:

  • Fake payment requests with urgency
  • Requests to share OTP/PIN for payments
  • Fake invoices with malicious links

Protection:

  • Never share OTPs or PINs
  • Verify payment requests independently
  • Check official payment channels
Check Payment Screen

Online Shopping Scams

Fake online stores that take your money but never deliver products, or deliver counterfeit items.

Warning signs:

  • Unrealistically low prices
  • Poor website design
  • Limited contact information
  • Only accepts wire transfers or crypto

Protection:

  • Research the store before buying
  • Use secure payment methods
  • Check for reviews from independent sources

Job Scams

Fake job offers that request payment or personal information, or promise unrealistic income.

Red flags:

  • Requests for payment to "secure" the job
  • Guaranteed high income with little work
  • Poor communication and unprofessional process
  • Requests for sensitive personal information

Protection:

  • Research the company thoroughly
  • Never pay for a job opportunity
  • Verify through official company channels
Check Job Message
📈

Investment Scams

Promises of guaranteed returns or unrealistic profits that are actually fraudulent schemes.

Warning signs:

  • Guaranteed returns with no risk
  • Pressure to invest quickly
  • Unregistered investment opportunities
  • Complex or confusing investment strategies

Protection:

  • If it sounds too good to be true, it probably is
  • Verify registration with financial authorities
  • Consult with registered financial advisors
Check Investment Offer
🎰

Lottery/Prize Scams

Notifications that you've won a lottery or prize, but require payment or personal information to claim it.

How it works:

  • You receive a message about winning a prize
  • Asked to pay fees or taxes to claim
  • Requested to provide personal information

Protection:

  • You can't win a lottery you didn't enter
  • Never pay to claim a prize
  • Verify through official lottery organizations

Cryptocurrency Scams

Fraudulent schemes involving fake crypto investments, fake exchanges, or requests for crypto payments.

Common types:

  • Fake crypto exchanges or wallets
  • Ponzi schemes disguised as crypto investments
  • Blackmail scams threatening to expose information
  • Fake celebrity endorsements

Protection:

  • Use reputable, regulated exchanges
  • Research before investing
  • Never share private keys or seed phrases

Fake Customer Support Scams

Scammers impersonate customer support to gain access to your accounts or personal information.

How it works:

  • Pop-ups claiming your computer has a virus
  • Calls from "tech support" about issues
  • Requests for remote access to your device
  • Asking for payment to fix non-existent issues

Protection:

  • Real companies don't make unsolicited support calls
  • Never give remote access to your device
  • Contact support through official channels only

Romance Scams

Scammers build fake romantic relationships to gain trust and eventually request money or personal information.

Warning signs:

  • Quick professing of love
  • Excuses for not meeting in person
  • Requests for money for emergencies
  • Inconsistencies in their stories

Protection:

  • Be skeptical of online relationships
  • Never send money to someone you haven't met
  • Verify their identity through video calls

OTP Scams

Scammers trick you into sharing One-Time Passwords (OTPs) to gain unauthorized access to your accounts.

How it works:

  • Scammers claim to need OTP for verification
  • Pretend to be from customer support
  • Use social engineering to obtain OTPs

Protection:

  • Never share OTPs with anyone
  • Legitimate organizations never ask for OTPs
  • If you receive an unexpected OTP, contact your bank
Check OTP Request
👤

Account Takeover Scams

Scammers gain access to your accounts through phishing, credential stuffing, or social engineering.

How it happens:

  • Phishing emails stealing login credentials
  • Using leaked passwords from other sites
  • Social engineering to reset passwords

Protection:

  • Use unique, strong passwords for each account
  • Enable two-factor authentication
  • Be suspicious of login requests

Delivery Scams

Fake delivery notifications that contain malicious links or request payment for non-existent packages.

Warning signs:

  • Unexpected delivery notifications
  • Requests for payment to release packages
  • Links to fake tracking websites
  • Poor grammar and spelling

Protection:

  • Track deliveries through official channels only
  • Don't click on links in unexpected messages
  • Verify with the sender through official contact
Check Delivery Message
🏛️

Fake Government Messages

Scammers impersonate government agencies to threaten or trick you into paying money or sharing information.

Common types:

  • Fake tax authority threats
  • False legal action threats
  • Impersonation of government officials
  • Fake government grant offers

Protection:

  • Government agencies don't threaten via phone/message
  • Verify through official government websites
  • Never make payments based on threatening messages

Universal Red Flags

Urgency

"Act now," "Immediate action required," "Last chance"

🚩

Threats

"Account will be closed," "Legal action," "Arrest warrant"

🚩

Too Good to Be True

Guaranteed returns, free money, unrealistic prizes

🚩

Requests for Sensitive Info

OTP, PIN, password, bank details, personal information

🚩

Unusual Payment Methods

Wire transfers, cryptocurrency, gift cards only

🚩

Poor Communication

Bad grammar, spelling errors, unprofessional language
