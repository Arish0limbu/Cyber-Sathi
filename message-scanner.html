Understanding Message Scams

Common Message Scams

  • OTP Scams: Requests to share OTP codes
  • Banking Scams: Fake account issues or payment requests
  • Prize Scams: Fake lottery or prize winnings
  • Job Scams: Fake job offers requesting payment
  • Investment Scams: Guaranteed returns on investments
  • Impersonation: Fake messages from known contacts

Red Flags to Watch For

  • Urgency ("Act now," "Immediate action required")
  • Threats ("Account will be closed," "Legal action")
  • Requests for personal information (OTP, PIN, password)
  • Too-good-to-be-true offers
  • Poor grammar and spelling
  • Suspicious links or attachments
  • Requests for payment or money transfers

How to Protect Yourself

  • Never share OTPs, PINs, or passwords
  • Verify senders through official channels
  • Don't click on suspicious links
  • Be skeptical of urgent requests
  • Report and block suspicious senders
  • Use message scanning tools when in doubt
