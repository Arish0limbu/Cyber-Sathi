Understanding URL Risks

What is a Suspicious URL?

Suspicious URLs may contain malicious content designed to steal your information or install harmful software. They often mimic legitimate websites but have subtle differences.

Common URL Threats

  • Phishing: Fake websites that steal credentials
  • Malware: Sites that download harmful software
  • Scams: Fraudulent sites designed to deceive
  • Redirects: URLs that redirect to malicious sites

How to Stay Safe

  • Always verify the domain name carefully
  • Look for HTTPS in the URL
  • Be cautious of shortened URLs
  • Don't click links from unknown sources
  • Use URL scanners before visiting suspicious links
