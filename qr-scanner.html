Understanding QR Code Risks

What are QR Code Scams?

Scammers use QR codes to hide malicious URLs. When you scan the code, it may direct you to phishing sites, download malware, or initiate unwanted payments.

Common QR Threats

  • Phishing URLs: Fake login pages that steal credentials
  • Payment Scams: QR codes that initiate unauthorized payments
  • Malware: Codes that download harmful software
  • Fake Apps: Redirects to malicious app downloads

How to Stay Safe

  • Always verify where a QR code leads before interacting
  • Be cautious of QR codes in public places
  • Don't scan QR codes from unknown sources
  • Use a QR scanner that shows the destination URL
  • Be suspicious of QR codes requesting payments
